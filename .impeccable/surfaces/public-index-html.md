## Scope

The rensel.dev home page (`public/index.html` with `styles.css` and `site.js`). The `/privacy` notice is outside scope and stays as it is.

Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action

- **Who:** potential clients, either software companies or Norwegian businesses, who arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, referrals or an email and decide within a minute whether to get in touch.
- **Action:** write to trond@rensel.dev ("Start a project"). LinkedIn and GitHub are secondary.
- **Proof:** the Tripletex role (general terms only), trmnl-api-mcp, svg-product-film, the Planloom film, and the Bluetree projects (Upstacked, Statens vegvesen).
- **Constraints:** English only. No company legal block. No AI-hype visuals. Short copy. All content present in the HTML without JavaScript.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is an assembly manual for AI that works inside real software. The visitor sees the parts, watches them go together, and knows who to call. It refuses the developer-portfolio default: dark hero, "Hi, I'm", a skill-icon grid, equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Uncoated manual paper and one black ink. Construction grey is for hidden lines and explosion lines. Part orange appears only on the part being added and on the place to act. Drawings are isometric 30° line art with circled part letters, dashed explosion lines with arrowheads, a detail circle and click marks. Type is one Norwegian grotesk with heavy numerals. No cards, boxes, shadows or gradients.

STORY: The visitor learns Trond is an AI engineer (Tripletex, shipping since 2018, Oslo). They see what he builds as parts, watch an AI system assemble onto their own systems, check the real work, and write to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A manual cover. Top strip: the Rensel wordmark on the left, contents links, and an orange "Get in touch" tab on the right. The left five columns hold "Trond Rensel" above a huge "AI engineer", a one-line promise, an orange "Start a project" button and a "See the work" link. The right seven columns hold an exploded isometric drawing of parts A–E (your data, retrieval, model, tools/MCP, your API) with a lettered legend. At the foot runs a full-width ink strip packed with facts.

FORM: Assembly Manual, candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list, assigned by the roll. seed:44672999212e82a2. Signature interaction: the assembly sequence. A sticky drawing assembles step by step as the visitor scrolls. Each part slides in along its explosion line, turns orange while it is the newest part, and lands with a click mark. Motion is mechanical slides along isometric axes with an exponential ease-out. Nothing fades in from invisible, and reduced motion shows each state without movement.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Raises from the declined challengers:

- Miura Fold Sheet: one isometric grid sets out every block.
- Indoor Sun: two inks plus part orange. The headshot is printed in ink on orange, and only the film plays in full colour.
- Deco Cinema Lobby: each section is denser than the last, running cover drawing, parts tray, assembly steps, spec table, then a one-line helpline.
- Obi Sleeve: the cover stays almost empty, and every fact goes into one dense ink strip along its foot.
- Cracktro Scroller: no cards or boxes. Only the manual's own devices frame content.
- Nixie Counter: every change of state is mechanical. Parts slide along their arrows and click.
